Identity Security Architecture Case Study

Zero Trust Identity Security Baseline

An Entra ID-focused baseline for strong authentication, least privilege, Conditional Access and privileged identity governance.

Entra IDConditional AccessMFAPIMIdentity ProtectionZero Trust
This is an architecture-oriented portfolio case study. It demonstrates design thinking and Microsoft identity-security knowledge rather than representing one customer's production configuration.

Security Objective

Reduce identity-driven risk by verifying users and devices explicitly, minimizing standing privilege and applying contextual access controls.

Core Principles

  • Verify explicitly
  • Use least privilege
  • Assume breach
  • Separate administration from daily-use identities

Baseline Architecture

1
Identity hygieneDefine joiner/mover/leaver lifecycle, group ownership, privileged roles and stale-account controls.
2
Strong authenticationRequire MFA, prefer phishing-resistant methods for privileged users and reduce legacy authentication.
3
Conditional AccessUse user, role, application, device, location and risk context to control access.
4
Privileged accessUse PIM, approval, time-bound activation, justification and access reviews to reduce standing privilege.
5
Identity riskUse sign-in and user-risk signals to drive investigation, remediation or stronger authentication.
6
Monitoring & governanceReview risky sign-ins, privileged-role activity, policy changes and exceptions through centralized security monitoring.

Policy Examples

  • Require MFA for all users
  • Block legacy authentication
  • Require compliant devices for sensitive apps
  • Stronger controls for admin roles
  • Session restrictions for unmanaged devices

Governance Controls

  • Access reviews
  • PIM activation history
  • Break-glass account monitoring
  • Conditional Access change control
  • Exception approval and expiry

What this demonstrates

The case study connects my SC-300 / AZ-500 / SC-100 knowledge with a practical identity-security operating model spanning technology, privilege governance and monitoring.
