01 / Business context

Governance starts with operational reality.

AIMG designs and manufactures heavy equipment across the USA, UAE, India, Europe and Asia. Its risk profile spans corporate technology, cloud platforms, enterprise applications and plant-floor systems.

Hybrid estate

IT and OT are governed as one business-risk landscape.

Production lines rely on MES, SCADA, ICS, PLCs, robotics, industrial networks and engineering workstations, while global operations depend on Azure, Entra ID, Microsoft 365, ERP, PLM, CRM and ServiceNow.

15,000 IT assets5,000 OT assets3 data centers~100 critical applications
Business dependency

Technology supports safety, production and revenue.

Cybersecurity decisions must consider production continuity, customer delivery, intellectual property, regulatory duties and operational safety—not vulnerability severity alone.

Accountability

Business ownership stays distinct from technical ownership.

Security and technology teams assess and advise. Business owners remain accountable for the outcomes, priorities and risk-treatment decisions of their processes.

03 / Impact model

Six lenses turn disruption into business language.

The framework evaluates financial, operational, customer, legal or regulatory, safety and reputational consequences with the relevant business owner.

04 / Crown jewels

Protect assets according to the process they enable.

Eight initial crown jewels are linked to business ownership and assessed for confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.

05 / Governance outcome

A repeatable line from strategy to security priority.

The model creates traceability: each protection priority can be tied back to an accountable business owner and a documented business consequence.

Next validation cycle

What remains deliberately open

  • RTO and RPO: validate recovery targets with business owners through a formal BIA.
  • Periodic review: revisit impact and crown-jewel status as operations and dependencies change.
  • Framework extension: link prioritized risks to controls, treatment plans, metrics and management reporting.
