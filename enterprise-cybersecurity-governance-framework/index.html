01 / Enterprise scope

Cybersecurity governance anchored in business impact.

The final framework covers corporate IT, Azure, Entra ID, enterprise applications, manufacturing OT/ICS, third-party dependencies and critical information assets.

Business criticality

Eight enterprise processes assessed.

Manufacturing, Finance, Supply Chain, Product Engineering, Sales, Email & Collaboration, IT Service Management and Human Resources are classified according to operational, financial, customer, regulatory, safety and reputation impact.

Crown jewels

Eight critical assets identified.

MES, Manufacturing OT/ICS, ERP, PLM, Engineering Design Repository, CRM, Microsoft Entra ID and Customer & Business Data are governed according to business dependency and CIA requirements.

Resilience

Recovery requirements are business-aligned.

Recovery tiers, RTO and RPO assumptions connect availability needs to business impact. In a real implementation, targets would be validated through formal Business Impact Analysis.

02 / Governance model

Clear authority from executive oversight to technical execution.

The framework separates business-risk ownership, security oversight, technical implementation and independent assurance.

Accountability

Business Owner + Risk Owner

Business Owners remain accountable for business outcomes. Risk Owners approve treatment or residual-risk acceptance within delegated authority and approved tolerance.

Oversight

Steering Committee + InfoSec

Cybersecurity governance identifies and assesses risk, recommends controls, monitors remediation, reports performance and escalates material issues without assuming ownership of business risk.

Assurance

Three Lines Model

Business and technology functions operate controls, cybersecurity/risk/compliance provide oversight and challenge, and Internal Audit remains independent from control ownership and remediation.

03 / Management governance

Risk decisions stay visible and measurable.

The framework extends beyond policy documentation into decision authority, escalation, metrics, assurance and continuous improvement.
