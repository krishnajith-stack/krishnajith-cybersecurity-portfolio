01 / Business context

Governance starts with operational reality.

AIMG designs and manufactures heavy equipment across the USA, UAE, India, Europe and Asia. Its risk profile spans corporate technology, cloud platforms, enterprise applications, identity services and plant-floor systems.

Hybrid estate

IT and OT are governed as one business-risk landscape.

Production relies on MES, SCADA, ICS, PLCs, robotics, industrial networks and engineering workstations, while global operations depend on Azure, Entra ID, Microsoft 365, ERP, PLM, CRM and ServiceNow.

15,000 IT assets5,000 OT assets3 data centers~100 critical applications
Business dependency

Technology supports safety, production and revenue.

Cybersecurity decisions consider production continuity, customer delivery, intellectual property, regulatory duties, financial impact and operational safety—not technical severity alone.

Accountability

Business ownership stays distinct from technical ownership.

Security and technology teams assess, advise and implement controls. Business and Risk Owners retain accountability for business-risk decisions within delegated authority.

A repeatable line from strategy to accountable action.

Governance validation

How the model stays credible

  • Recovery targets: validate portfolio RTO/RPO assumptions through formal BIA with business and continuity stakeholders.
  • Periodic review: reassess process criticality, Crown Jewels, CIA needs and recovery requirements as operations and dependencies change.
  • Management oversight: use risk ownership, RACI, metrics, assurance and annual governance review to keep decisions traceable and accountable.
